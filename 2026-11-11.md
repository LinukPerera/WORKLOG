## PaliVerse RAG — mechanical connection-filtering pipeline built and validated, a three-model (Haiku/Sonnet/Opus) comparison run twice with manual adjudication, full chapter-1 coverage confirmed and a real multi-chunk bug fixed, then generalized to the whole canon and moved onto the Message Batch API — 4 hours (10:30–14:30)

Source: `mini_rag/WORKLOG.MD`, referred to here as "PaliVerse RAG"

**Built a mechanical (no LLM call) content-overlap check, `content_jaccard()`** in
`find_related_passages.py`, to solve the problem flagged 09/11 - a low embedding
distance alone can't tell a formulaic-only match from a genuinely useful one.
Validated on the two known examples first (genuine match 0.771 vs known-bad
formulaic match 0.075, a 10x gap) before trusting it further. Checking it
against more real results found two real false flags (same-text enumeration
siblings, a paraphrased commentary passage) - fixed the first by giving
same-text matches a lower threshold (0.10 vs cross-text's 0.25), left the
second open rather than tune on a sample of one.

**Added a second mechanical signal, `proper_noun_overlap()`**, using NLTK's POS
tagger (installed fresh this session) - Jaccard overlap of proper nouns only.
This fixed the remaining false flag (the paraphrase case named the same two
characters as the source unit, 0.250 proper-noun overlap vs a diluted 0.087
word-level score), while keeping the known-bad case correctly flagged (0.059).
Wired in as an OR condition; re-ran all three known cases plus 9 more sample
units to find the real uncertain remainder (2 cases, both looking like genuine
borderline calls, not bugs).

**Built `verify_flagged_connections.py`** - a real Claude call scoped only to
whatever survives the mechanical filter still `FLAGGED`, reusing the existing
`ClaudeClient`. Confirmed on the 2 remainder cases from above: both correctly
`NOT RELATED`, matching the earlier manual read - the layered design (free
mechanical filter, LLM only on the small remainder) working end to end.

**Built `validate_mechanical_sample.py`** to get a real, regular-sized result
instead of ad-hoc spot checks - caught and fixed a real bug in it first (missing
the `chapter_id` filter, so it reported 559 units instead of chapter 1's real
149). Real result over a stratified 30-unit sample: **143/150 GENUINE (95.3%),
7 FLAGGED (4.7%), 0 WEAK-MATCH**.

**Built `compare_verification_models.py` and ran Haiku vs Sonnet on the 6 real
FLAGGED pairs from that sample** - only 2/6 agreed (33%). Manually read every
disagreement rather than just counting votes: Sonnet was right on 3 (a shared
distinctive "seed-kingdom" vocabulary list Haiku missed entirely), Haiku right
on 1 (an equanimity-toward-praise-and-blame connection Sonnet dismissed). Picked
**Sonnet** as the production default - missing a real connection is the costlier
failure mode for an info-tab use case than one defensible miss.

**Built `ensure_all_units_connected.py` and ran the full pipeline across all 149
chapter-1 units** - 27 Sonnet calls, 13 promoted to GENUINE, but **1 unit (24)
came back with zero validated connections.** Traced this to a real bug repeated
across three scripts: each used `ORDER BY chunk_index LIMIT 1`, silently reading
only a unit's first chunk - for unit 24 that's literally just the text "24.".
Checked the real scope first: **9 of 149 units** span multiple chunks and were
all affected. Fixed with one shared `get_unit_representation()` helper
(concatenates all chunks' text, averages+renormalizes their embeddings) used by
all three scripts instead of each duplicating the broken query. Re-ran only the
9 affected units to confirm - all 9 now have genuine connections. **Full
coverage confirmed for chapter 1.**

own related-passages feature, but hardcoded to the Dīgha Nikāya volume only and
structurally unable to reach the cross-Nikāya connections just validated. Plan

JSON export - explicitly flagged the regenerate-existing-149-vs-apply-going-
forward choice as the user's to make, not a technical call.

**Smoke-tested the generalized pipeline on a different book (`abh01m.mul`,
